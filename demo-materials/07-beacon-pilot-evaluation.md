# Beacon pilot evaluation

Forty support agents participated in a task-based pilot with 186 evaluation
tasks drawn from historical, de-identified support scenarios. The evaluated
outcome was time to find an approved knowledge article, not end-to-end ticket
resolution. The baseline and prototype used the same task definitions.

Median time to find an approved answer decreased from 4.2 minutes to 3.1
minutes, a reduction of 1.1 minutes, or approximately 26.2%. The observed pilot
improvement was below the approximately 35% design target. We do not have
evidence of production-wide time savings, revenue or customer-satisfaction
changes.

Alex Chen supplied the prototype, evaluation harness and analysis worksheet.
Support Operations supplied tasks and evaluated whether answers were approved.
Morgan Patel coordinated the production integration. The pilot result reflects
the team's combined work, rather than Alex's contribution alone.

Recommended follow-up: evaluate article freshness and zero-result queries
before expanding the pilot. Expansion has not yet been completed.
