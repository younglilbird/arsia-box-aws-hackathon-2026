# Atlas design: guided workspace onboarding

## Audience and problem

Atlas serves first-time workspace administrators at small and midsize customer
organizations. Existing onboarding has disconnected setup screens. The
baseline median time to finish the defined first-workspace setup task is 10
minutes. Completing setup does not measure long-term retention or revenue.

## Scope and success target

The project replaces three setup screens with one guided checklist and clearer
validation. The proposed success target is a 30% reduction in median setup
time, from 10 to 7 minutes. This is a design target, not a measured outcome.
Enterprise migration, billing changes and mobile onboarding are out of scope.

## Responsibilities

Priya Shah is project lead and coordinates the cross-functional release.
Mateo Ruiz owns the guided UI implementation. Alex Chen owns measurement:
the event taxonomy, instrumentation, data-quality checks and pilot verification.
Alex will collaborate with Design and Data Science to define completion and
drop-off consistently. The project has not launched as of this design date.
